# Source attribution and reuse

The observational values in this catalogue were compiled from the studies and databases identified in `references.bib` and `data/source_references.csv`. Their inclusion here does not change the rights or attribution requirements associated with the original sources.

A dataset-wide reuse license has not yet been specified. The availability of these files does not itself grant a blanket license over third-party publications, databases, figures, or other source material.

Users should acknowledge both this compilation and the studies or databases supplying the values they use.
